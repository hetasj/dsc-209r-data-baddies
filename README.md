# Exploring Gender Gaps in Upper-Secondary Educational Attainment
 
DSC 209R Project 3: Interactive Visualization
 
**Team Data Baddies:** Safia Ulysse, Jade Romero, Heta Joshi

## Research Question
 
How do gender gaps in upper-secondary educational attainment vary across countries with different GDP-per-capita levels, and how have these gaps changed over time from 2010 to 2018?
 
## Data
 
We use World Bank indicators to explore how gender differences in educational attainment vary across countries with different economic contexts and how these differences have changed over time.
 
**Countries:** Brazil, Colombia, Dominican Republic, Ecuador, Mexico, United States, Uruguay
 
**Years:** 2010 to 2018
 
**Variables:**
- Educational attainment, at least completed upper secondary, population 25+, female (%)
- Educational attainment, at least completed upper secondary, population 25+, male (%)
- Educational attainment, at least completed upper secondary, population 25+, total (%)
- GDP per capita (constant 2010 US$)
- Government expenditure on education, total (% of GDP)
- Government expenditure per student, secondary (% of GDP per capita)
