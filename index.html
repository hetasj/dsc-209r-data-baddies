Exploring Gender Gaps in Upper-Secondary Educational Attainment

By: Jade, Heta and Safia

Research Question: How is government expenditure per secondary student related to gender gaps in upper-secondary educational attainment across the United States, Europe, and Latin America between 2000 and 2018?

Project Description: In this project, we explore gender gaps in upper-secondary educational attainment across 14 countries: the United States, six European countries, and seven Latin American countries. Using data from the World Bank, we investigate differences between female and male educational attainment and how these gaps relate to government spending on secondary education.

Inspired by Mike Bostock’s The Wealth & Health of Nations interactive bubble chart, we will create a visualization that allows users to explore changes over time, from 2000 to 2018. Each circle represents a country, with its size representing GDP per capita. The x-axis shows government expenditure per secondary student as a percentage of GDP per capita, while the y-axis shows the gender gap in upper-secondary educational attainment.
